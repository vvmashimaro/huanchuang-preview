About Huanchuang

Built on Industry Experience. Developed Through Research.

Established in Shanghai in 2014, Huanchuang began with real estate project investment, developing an understanding of regional property markets, project development and related industries. In 2018, the company expanded its investment activities into China's A-share market, extending its research to the real estate value chain and related listed companies. This development broadened its research capabilities across real estate projects, industry analysis and corporate investment assessment. We believe that understanding underlying industries and business operations is fundamental to informed, independent investment judgment.
