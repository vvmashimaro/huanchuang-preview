About us

Experience shapes
our perspective.

Shanghai Huanchuang Investment Management Co., Ltd. focuses on real estate project equity investment and China’s domestic A-share market.

A Shanghai company.
A regional foundation.

Huanchuang was founded in 2014 with a focus on real estate project investment in Shanghai and surrounding areas, and Chengdu and surrounding areas in Sichuan.

The company’s real estate activities draw on experience in property development and project management. Selected projects involve joint investment and management alongside local developers.

Since 2018, Huanchuang has also invested its own capital in the A-share secondary market, using market research and listed-company analysis to inform investment decisions.

Our development

Two defining chapters.

2014

A real estate foundation

Established in Shanghai, with local real estate project investment as the company’s initial focus.

2018

A broader investment focus

Added China’s A-share secondary-market investment alongside the existing real estate business.
