Real Estate

Regional real estate markets.

Huanchuang invests in commercial and residential development projects in Shanghai and surrounding areas, and Chengdu and surrounding areas in Sichuan.

The company participates through project equity investment. In selected projects, it works with local developers under joint investment and project management arrangements, drawing on experience in development and project delivery.

  3. Joint management

    Selected projects co-invested and co-managed with local developers.

A-Share
Equity Investment

Research informs judgment.

Since 2018, Huanchuang has invested its own capital in China’s domestic A-share secondary market.

Investment decisions are informed by assessments of market conditions and research into specific listed companies. The focus is on understanding the market context and the businesses under consideration.

A closer look.

Where does the real estate business focus?

The business focuses on commercial and residential development projects in Shanghai and surrounding areas, and Chengdu and surrounding areas in Sichuan.

How does Huanchuang participate in real estate projects?

Through project equity investment, with selected projects involving joint investment and management alongside local real estate developers.

What does the A-share business involve?

Investment of the company’s own capital in China’s domestic A-share secondary market, informed by market and listed-company research. This website does not offer third-party investment management services.
